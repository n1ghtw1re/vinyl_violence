Orbital - Orbital 2 (The Brown Album)

Published: April 17, 2025 | By Vinyl Violence

Following their influential debut (the "Green Album"), the Hartnoll brothers returned in 1993 with their second self-titled album, affectionately known as the "Brown Album" due to its artwork. While retaining the core elements of their sound, Orbital 2 pushed further into lush, melodic, and atmospheric techno territories, crafting a sophisticated and emotionally resonant electronic journey that became a cornerstone of the burgeoning "intelligent techno" movement.

Expanding the Palette

Where the Green Album often focused on raw rave energy, the Brown Album demonstrated a more refined and expansive sonic palette. Phil and Paul Hartnoll expertly blended intricate rhythms, warm analog synth textures, ethereal pads, and carefully chosen vocal samples to create tracks that were both danceable and deeply immersive. The production felt richer, more layered, and more focused on melody and atmosphere.

The album moves seamlessly between moments of uplifting euphoria, introspective melancholy, and driving techno energy. It showcased Orbital's growing confidence as composers, capable of crafting complex, evolving electronic pieces that transcended simple dance track structures.

Iconic Tracks, Enduring Emotion

Orbital 2 is perhaps best known for the timeless classic "Halcyon On and On." Built around an Ophélie Winter sample and dedicated to the Hartnolls' mother (who was addicted to the tranquilizer Halcion), the track is a masterpiece of bittersweet euphoria, blending blissful melodies with a subtle undercurrent of sadness. It remains one of electronic music's most beloved and emotionally resonant anthems.

The album is stacked with other highlights. "Lush 3-1" and "Lush 3-2" form a stunning, propulsive techno suite. "Impact (The Earth Is Burning)" delivers relentless, environmentally conscious energy. "Remind" offers intricate, evolving rhythms, while the opening track "Time Becomes" (often starting live sets) sets a hypnotic, spacey tone. The beautiful, ambient closing track "Attached" provides a perfect, reflective conclusion.

A Landmark in Electronic Music

The Brown Album solidified Orbital's reputation as pioneers of intelligent, emotive electronic music. It garnered critical acclaim and helped bring sophisticated techno to a wider audience, proving that electronic music could be both intellectually stimulating and profoundly moving.

Its influence can still be heard today, and tracks like "Halcyon" remain staples. It's a beautifully crafted, timeless album that perfectly captures the spirit of early 90s electronic music innovation. Essential listening for any electronic music enthusiast.
