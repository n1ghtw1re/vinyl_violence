Deep Dish - Global Underground 021 Moscow

Published: April 15, 2025 | By Vinyl Violence

The Global Underground series captured snapshots of legendary DJs playing in iconic cities worldwide. Few entries resonated as deeply or proved as influential as Deep Dish's 2001 mix from Moscow (GU021). This double-disc journey showcased Dubfire and Sharam at the height of their progressive house powers, crafting a seamless, dark, and driving narrative that defined the sound for many.

The Sound of Progressive Peak Time

GU021 Moscow is a masterclass in tension and release. Disc 1 builds slowly, layering hypnotic rhythms, deep basslines, and atmospheric textures. Tracks like Morel's "True (The Faggot Is You)" (in the Dub version) and Luzon's "The Baguio Track" set a moody, tribal tone before gradually introducing more melodic and uplifting elements. Disc 2 picks up the energy, moving into peak-time territory with driving percussion, soaring synth lines, and epic breakdowns, featuring classics like iiO's "Rapture" (in the Deep Dish Space Mix) and the unforgettable energy of Situation 2wo's "Way2tite."

The mixing is impeccable throughout, with long, smooth blends that allow tracks to breathe and build momentum organically. Deep Dish expertly navigated the space between deep, tribal rhythms and soaring progressive melodies, creating a sound that was both sophisticated and intensely powerful on the dance floor.

A Personal Catalyst

This mix holds a particularly special place for me. **Even though I was already raving and deeply immersed in the electronic music scene for a while, GU021 Moscow was *the* mix CD that truly flipped the switch and inspired me to get behind the decks myself.** The way Dubfire and Sharam controlled the energy, curated the journey, and blended tracks seamlessly was just captivating. It wasn't just about the individual tracks; it was about the story they told over two hours. It made me want to learn how to do *that*.

Enduring Influence

Deep Dish's Moscow mix became a benchmark for progressive house compilations. It captured a specific moment in time when the genre was arguably at its peak popularity and creative energy. The track selection, the flow, and the overall dark, driving atmosphere influenced countless DJs and producers.

Listening back today, GU021 retains its power. It's a timeless journey through the depths of progressive house, expertly crafted by two masters of the genre. An essential mix for anyone interested in the history of electronic dance music.
