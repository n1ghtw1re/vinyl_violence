About Vinyl Violence

Welcome to Vinyl Violence, the digital space where raw energy meets sonic exploration. This blog delves into the worlds of punk rock, underground techno, hip-hop history (especially pioneers like the Beastie Boys), and anything else that hits hard and pushes boundaries.

Expect deep dives into classic albums, thoughts on emerging scenes, explorations of the culture surrounding the music, and maybe the occasional rant. This isn't about polished reviews; it's about the feeling, the impact, and the stories behind the sounds.

This site itself is an experiment in minimalism – built with just HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. No frameworks, no backend bloat, just fast-loading content. Like a good punk track: lean, mean, and straight to the point.

Stick around, turn it up loud.
